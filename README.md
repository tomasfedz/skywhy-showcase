# SkyWhy

**Weather, and why.**

> A weather experience for people who actually want to understand what they’re looking at.

---

## What is SkyWhy?

Most weather apps give you numbers.  
SkyWhy gives you the numbers **and** the reason behind them.

- Clean current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecast
- Small `*` markers next to values that open short, accurate scientific explainers (dew point vs humidity, pressure at altitude, feels-like, etc.)
- Dynamic ribbons that surface real atmospheric and orbital events (perihelion, solstice timing, frost risk, rapid pressure drops…)

The goal is not more data.  
The goal is **understanding**.

---

## Why this exists

Weather apps are optimized for glancing.  
SkyWhy is optimized for the moment you look at a number and think *“wait… why?”*

It started as a personal project focused on Rexburg, Idaho, and grew into a portfolio product that combines:

- Real meteorological data (Open-Meteo)
- Careful scientific explanations
- Clean product design
